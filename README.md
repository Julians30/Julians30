## About me

I am an agricultural economist and lecturer-researcher in the Agricultural Science program at the **Universidad Técnica de Machala**, Ecuador. I develop and evaluate artificial intelligence methods for agricultural production and food quality, with a focus on **computer vision**, **near-infrared (NIR) spectroscopy** and **machine learning**.

My work centers on rigorous model evaluation: partitions that respect the experimental unit, leakage prevention, calibration, uncertainty, domain shift and reproducibility. As an economist, I also aim to quantify the costs, benefits and productive effects of these technologies.
